The course in six formulas

\( r=\dfrac{S_{xy}}{\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}} \)correlation
\( t=\dfrac{r\sqrt{n-2}}{\sqrt{1-r^2}} \)is r significant?
\( \hat\beta=(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y \)OLS
\( R^2=1-\dfrac{RSS}{TSS} \)fit
\( F=\dfrac{\Delta RSS/q}{RSS/df} \)compare models
\( AIC=2k-2\ln L \)model choice
What these notes are built from

The R scripts in each lecture folder (Lec 1.R, Lec 2 modeling.R, Lec 3___missing.R, Robust Regression.R), the two Lecture 3 handouts (Correlation Coefficient, Linear Regression and OLS), and the datasets. Every script was re-run in R 4.6, and the outputs and plots you see are from those runs. Lecture 5 also includes points the professor made in the lecture videos; for Lectures 1–4 the videos weren't used, so anything said only in class won't be there.
